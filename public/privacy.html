Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

etc. ("we") is a small snow shovelling and odd-jobs business in Calgary, Alberta. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We follow Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email, phone number and service address, from the booking form, a call, a text or an email.
  • Job details: the work you asked for, notes (like gate codes), visits, quotes, invoices and payments.
  • Photos: before and after photos of the work, and photos you send us for a quote (by text or upload). We remove location data from photos you upload.
  • Messages: texts and emails between you and us.

How we use it

  • To schedule and do the work, send quotes, invoices and receipts, and answer your questions.
  • To send texts about your booking (see Text messages below).
  • To keep business and tax records.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to texts (on the booking form, or by texting us first), we send texts about your booking: when we're on the way, when the job is done (with photos), quotes, invoices and replies to your messages. Phones that support it get these as RCS (branded chat with read receipts and reply buttons); others get regular SMS/MMS. Message frequency varies with your bookings, usually a few texts per job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP any time to stop texts, START to turn them back on, and HELP for help. We don't share your phone number or text consent with anyone for their own marketing.

Marketing emails and texts

We may occasionally email clients about seasonal services (for example, spring cleanup or snow plans) if you asked for offers, or if you have bought from us in the last two years or asked us about work in the last six months, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) allows. Marketing texts only go to people who agreed to texts. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text. We note when marketing emails are opened or links are clicked so we know what's useful.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the business, each for its own job:

  • Telnyx (text messages, including RCS through Google's RCS service)
  • Brevo (email delivery) and Cloudflare (website and email routing)
  • Google Maps (address lookup on the booking form)
  • Stripe (card payments, if you pay by card)

Our customer records are kept on our own server in Calgary. Some providers above may process information outside Canada.

Photos

Job photos are private. We only show a photo on our website if you've told us that's OK, and you can change your mind any time.

How long we keep it

As long as you're a client, and then as long as needed for tax records (generally six years for invoices and payments).

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct your information, stop texts or emails, or have us delete what we don't need to keep for tax records. Email help@etccrew.ca or call 403-656-8200. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
