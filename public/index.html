Why "etc."?

Hi neighbours, I'm Silas 👋

I've been shovelling snow and fixing things with my Dad for more than seven years. Somewhere along the way I figured out what I want to do: become an electrician.

Trade school costs real money, so my friends and I started etc. — snow shovelling, bin cleaning, windows, leaves, junk runs, et cetera. If it's an honest job around your house, we'll do it.

When you hire us, you're not just getting a clean driveway. You're helping a kid from down the street start a career. Thank you for that. Seriously.

Questions

Good to know

Do you come to my neighbourhood?

Most likely. We're based in Evergreen and cover southwest and south Calgary, plus lake, estate and inner-city communities like Lake Bonavista, Mount Royal, Elbow Park, Britannia, Aspen Woods and Springbank Hill. See the full list under Service area, or just call and ask.

Where does the money go?

After gas and supplies, it goes into Silas's savings for electrician school. The tracker at the top of this page shows how close he is.

How soon do you come after it snows?

Every-snowfall customers come first, usually the same day the snow stops. One-time bookings are done as soon as we can get there — we'll give you a time when you call.

Is an adult involved?

Silas is 18, but he's getting lots of support with his new venture. Dad has worked alongside him for 7+ years and still helps with the bigger jobs like roofs, ladders and repairs.

Are taxes included?

Yes. All prices include tax, so $35 is $35. No surprises on the bill.

How do I pay?

After the job, by cash or Interac e-Transfer to the email we send with your booking confirmation.

Can I cancel the every-snowfall plan?

Anytime. Just give us a call. No contracts.
