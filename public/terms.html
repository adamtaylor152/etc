Terms of service

Last updated November 27, 2026

These terms cover booking and paying for work from etc. ("we"), a small snow shovelling and odd-jobs business in Calgary, Alberta, and our text messages. By booking with us, you agree to them.

The work

  • Snow shovelling covers the driveway, front walk and steps unless we agree otherwise. Every-snowfall plans are done after each snowfall that reaches the plan's trigger depth.
  • Other jobs are quoted first. We start once you approve the quote.
  • Weather, safety and big storms can change timing. We'll let you know if we're delayed.
  • Snow and ice can come back after we leave (more snow, melting and refreezing, wind). Please use care on walkways; ice melt is available as an add-on.

Prices and payment

  • Prices on our website and quotes include any tax. We're not registered for GST.
  • You pay after the job, by cash, Interac e-Transfer (to the address on your invoice, with the invoice code in the message) or card when offered. Invoices are due on receipt unless the invoice says otherwise.

Cancelling

Cancel or pause any time by text, call or email. There's no charge for work we haven't started.

Photos

We take before and after photos of our work for your records and ours. See the privacy policy for how we handle them.

Text messaging terms

  • Program: etc. booking texts, about your bookings: on-the-way and done notices (with photos), quotes, invoices, receipts and replies to your messages.
  • How to join: tick the text box on the booking form, tell us you'd like texts, or text us at 403-656-8200.
  • How often: varies with your bookings, usually a few texts per job.
  • Cost: we don't charge for texts. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.
  • Stop or get help: reply STOP to stop texts (we'll confirm once), START to restart, HELP for help, or contact help@etccrew.ca / 403-656-8200.
  • RCS: phones that support RCS get our texts as branded chat messages with read receipts and reply buttons; other phones get SMS/MMS.
  • Carriers aren't responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.
