# Jobber: invoicing, payments, client hub, communications, marketing, automation, AI, reporting, integrations (as of late November 2026)

Research notes for a Jobber-parity CRM aimed at a small residential snow-removal / yard-care crew in Calgary, Canada. Primary source is the Jobber Help Center (help.getjobber.com, new `/en/articles/<slug>` URL scheme), read article by article on 2026-11-27/28, plus the getjobber.com pricing and feature pages. Country flags used below: **[US-only]**, **[CA ok]** (confirmed available in Canada), **[CA differs]** (available but behaves differently in Canada).

Source-quality notes that apply throughout:
- Help Center articles were read through a summarising fetcher, so wording is close paraphrase, not verbatim. Numbers (limits, days, fees) are as the fetcher reported them from the article.
- `developer.getjobber.com`, `apps.getjobber.com/app_marketplace` and `productupdates.getjobber.com` all returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher. API/webhook and marketplace-catalogue facts therefore come from the Help Center, search snippets, or third-party pages and are flagged where used.
- The pricing page was served in USD. Canadian-dollar plan prices and Canadian card-processing rates were not confirmed.

## 1. Invoicing: creation paths, batch invoicing, reminders/follow-ups, terms, late fees, statuses, partial payments, deposits, credits/balance, refunds, taxes, discounts, statements, bad debt, templates/PDF

### Takeaway
Jobber's invoicing is a job-driven pipeline: jobs generate "invoice reminders" that push the job into a **Requires Invoicing** status, from which invoices are created singly, per client, or in batch (always as drafts), then batch-delivered by email or printed for mail with address labels. Around that sit six statuses (Draft, Awaiting Payment, Past Due, Paid, Bad Debt, Voided), per-client payment terms, a client account balance with deposits/credits/initial balance, client statements, tax groups, global and line-item discounts, progress-invoicing payment schedules, and two automated past-due follow-ups. There is no native automatic late-fee engine that I could find; Jobber offers a "late stamp", a disclaimer field and follow-up messages instead.

### Cited Findings

**Creation paths**
- Four ways to create an invoice: (1) the global Create button, giving a standalone invoice not linked to a job (no job details, line items added manually, no automatic property address); (2) from the client profile, combining any of that client's Requires Invoicing jobs you tick into one invoice; (3) from an invoice reminder on the Schedule or the job's Billing > Reminders tab; (4) from the job itself (Create Invoice if it is in Requires Invoicing, otherwise More > Create Invoice). — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- When invoicing from a job, service dates, service address, line items, discount, tax rate and any collected deposit carry over; other Requires Invoicing jobs for the same client can be added on the next screen. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Jobs on properties with different tax rates cannot be combined on one invoice; Jobber shows "You can't generate an invoice for properties with different tax rates." — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- "Create Similar Invoice" copies line items, subject, message, disclaimer, discount and custom fields to a new draft. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Past invoices can be imported by CSV. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)

**Invoice fields and limits**
- Header fields: client, subject (defaults from work settings), invoice number, payment terms, issued date (defaults to the send date), salesperson, custom fields. Invoice numbers must be numeric only and auto-increment from the highest existing number. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Maximum 100 line items per invoice. Each line has name (required), description, quantity, unit price, computed total, an optional per-line service date, and a taxable/non-taxable flag that is managed in the Products & Services list. Lines can be drag-reordered. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Per-invoice "Client view" switches: show/hide quantities, unit prices, line totals, account balance and the late stamp. Account-wide defaults live in Business Profile > Client document settings and do not retroactively change existing documents. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Per-invoice client hub payment switches: card payments, bank (ACH) payments, partial payments (off by default). **[CA differs]** Canadian accounts accept card only, so those method toggles are not shown. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Client message can be rewritten by AI in cheerful, casual, professional or shorter tone. Contract/disclaimer text defaults from client document settings, can be customised per invoice, with an "Apply to all future invoices" option. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Images and attachments on invoices: Grow, Plus or Marketing Suite; web only (not the app); up to 10 files per section; images show in client hub; attachments are listed by filename in the PDF with a link to client hub. Plus adds a "Files from related work" tab. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Invoices have an activity history (clock icon) filterable by team, type and date, showing before/after values for edits plus deposits and payments. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Collect Signature on an invoice saves a signed PDF to the invoice's notes and attachments. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)

**Statuses**
- Draft: created, not yet sent or marked sent. Awaiting Payment: sent, unpaid, not yet due. Past Due: past its due date and unpaid. Paid: full balance paid, or manually marked paid. Bad Debt: part or all deemed uncollectible. Voided: cancelled; cannot be edited, sent or paid but is retained. — [Invoices list page and key metrics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoices-list-page-and-key-metrics)
- Sending by email or text (or marking as sent) moves a draft out of Draft. Payments can be collected on draft invoices. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Close Invoice has three variants: with a payment; as bad debt (writes off remaining balance only); without a payment (marks Paid but leaves the balance owing on the account, intended for people who track balances outside Jobber). — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Edge case: a reopened invoice with $0 balance can still show Awaiting Payment or Past Due; the fix is Close invoice > Without recording a payment. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Voiding: only unpaid invoices; not available for progress-invoicing schedule invoices or Wisetack-financed invoices; reasons are Duplicate, Created in error, Client request; voided invoices are immutable; any attached deposit returns to the client's balance; the client gets email and SMS notice. Jobber's guidance is void for mistakes, bad debt for valid-but-uncollectible work. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- Invoice list page: filter by status and by due date (All, This month, Last month, This year, Custom); sortable by client, invoice number, due date, status, total, balance. Key metrics: status overview, Issued (count and value, last 30 days vs prior 30), Average invoice price (last 30 days), Average time to get paid (invoices paid in the last 7 days, issued to paid). — [Invoices list page and key metrics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoices-list-page-and-key-metrics)

**Invoice reminders and the Requires Invoicing status**
- Invoice reminders are calendar prompts; when one comes due the job moves to Requires Invoicing, which is surfaced as a count on Home and a status filter on the Jobs page. — [Invoice reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-reminders)
- Recurring jobs choose a billing type (Per visit, or Fixed price regardless of visit count) and an invoicing schedule: "As needed - we won't prompt you" (no reminders); After each visit (reminder when each visit is completed); Once when job is completed; Monthly on the last day of the month; Custom schedule. — [Invoice reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-reminders)
- One-off jobs get a reminder when the job closes by default; untick the billing checkbox to skip. Manual reminders can be added from the job's Billing > Reminders tab. — [Invoice reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-reminders)
- All invoice reminders can be assigned to one user (for example a billing person) in Work Settings. — [Invoice reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-reminders)
- A closed job stays in Requires Invoicing until the overdue reminder is cleared by creating the invoice or deleting the reminder; if no invoice is needed the job then moves to Archived. — [Invoice reminders](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-reminders)

**Batch invoicing (two steps)**
- Batch Create (Invoices > More Actions > Batch Create Invoices): tick clients, then the visits to include; an "All" filter selects every client and job; a side panel summarises how many invoices will be created. One invoice per client, combining that client's jobs; split into multiple invoices when properties have different tax rates. — [Batch create invoices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/batch-create-invoices)
- Incomplete visits (past or future) are excluded by default; selecting one manually marks it complete. All batch-created invoices are saved as Drafts. Batch creation only works for jobs that have invoice reminders set, on or after the reminder date; the reminders are then marked completed. — [Batch create invoices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/batch-create-invoices)
- Batch Deliver (Invoices > More Actions > Batch Deliver Invoices): choose email or standard mail; Draft, Awaiting Payment and Past Due invoices are selectable. Email goes to the client's primary (starred) email and their billing contact; the template is editable before Send Now; drafts become Awaiting Payment. The invoice PDF is not attached unless the "PDF of invoice" box is ticked. — [Batch deliver invoices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/batch-deliver-invoices)
- Standard mail delivery emails you one printable PDF of all selected invoices plus a mailing-label document for billing addresses (Avery 5160, Avery 7160, or #10 envelope); generation can take up to 15 minutes; paid invoices cannot be selected. — [Batch deliver invoices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/batch-deliver-invoices)
- The batch-deliver article does not offer text message as a batch channel. — [Batch deliver invoices](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/batch-deliver-invoices)
- A dedicated help article covers a snow-removal workflow for scheduling new visits across many jobs at once (title only; content not read here). — [Work topic index](https://help.getjobber.com/en/topics/work/)

**Payment terms and due dates**
- Built-in terms: Due upon receipt, Net 7, Net 15, Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, End of the month, End of next month. Custom terms (name + number of days) can be added, renamed, deleted. The three non-Net built-ins cannot be renamed, deleted or changed. — [Set invoice payment terms](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/set-invoice-payment-terms)
- Two type defaults (residential, which also covers clients with no type; and commercial, meaning clients entered with a company name), plus an optional per-client override. Resolution order when an invoice is created: client's individual term, then the type default. Terms can be overridden on a single invoice with a checkbox to save it as that client's new default. Deleting a term that is a default falls back to Due upon receipt. — [Set invoice payment terms](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/set-invoice-payment-terms)

**Invoice follow-ups (automated dunning)**
- Two invoice follow-up automations, each sending one reminder for invoices in Past Due status, timed as N days after the due date (up to 90 days), sent shortly after 8 AM local time, on the same channel (email or text) as the original invoice, each with a one-click payment option. The client or contact must have "overdue invoice follow-ups" enabled in their communication settings. Plans: Connect, Grow, Plus. — [Automations](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automations)
- Follow-up templates can use `{{DAYS_OVERDUE}}`, `{{INVOICE_BALANCE}}`, `{{INVOICE_DUE_DATE}}`, `{{INVOICE_NUMBER}}`, `{{INVOICE_TOTAL}}`, `{{ACCOUNT_BALANCE}}`. — [Variables](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/variables)
- Grow/Plus can add further dunning via the custom automation builder (for example, "30 days after invoices are due, tag the client non-payment", or extra notify steps). — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)

**Late fees**
- The payment-terms article does not mention late fees. — [Set invoice payment terms](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/set-invoice-payment-terms)
- Invoice PDFs can show a "late stamp" on overdue invoices (toggle in client document settings and per invoice). — [Client document settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-document-settings)
- In a Jobber Community thread, a user reported support telling them automatic late fees/interest were not available and manual line items were the only option; other users described adding the fee as a line item. Undated in the search result, so may be stale. — [Jobber Community: auto add late fees/interest](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/finances-forum/auto-add-late-feesinterest/917)
- Jobber Academy's late-fee guidance recommends a grace period and a disclaimer that applies to all outgoing invoices, i.e. policy text, not a computed fee. — [Jobber Academy: late fees on invoices](https://getjobber.com/academy/late-fees-on-invoices/)

**Collecting payment, partial payments**
- Collect Payment offers Jobber Payments card (button reads "Charge") or recorded offline methods (button reads "Save" / "Save and Email Receipt"); a separate "card" record type logs a card processed elsewhere without charging. Each payment has transaction date, method-specific fields (for example cheque number) and notes. — [How to collect payment on an invoice](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/how-to-collect-payment-on-an-invoice)
- Offline methods listed on the client billing screen: cash, cheque, bank transfer, money order, other. The Insights payment-method breakdown additionally shows "E-transfer" as a type. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history); [Insights dashboard](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/insights-dashboard)
- Partial payments: staff simply edit the amount; many partials can apply to one invoice; status does not change until paid in full. In client hub, partial payments are off by default and enabled per invoice; not available on progress invoices. — [How to collect payment on an invoice](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/how-to-collect-payment-on-an-invoice)
- From the client's billing screen a single "bulk payment" can cover several outstanding invoices (Total / Balance / Payment columns, "Select all"); it appears in history as "payment applied to account balance". — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- A payment cannot be collected against the bare account balance when no invoice exists; create an invoice or record a deposit instead. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)

**Deposits and how they apply**
- A quote can carry "Deposit only" (percentage or fixed amount due on approval) or a full "Payment schedule". A quote must be sent (out of Draft) before a deposit can be collected. — [Deposits on quotes](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/deposits-on-quotes)
- Client hub shows "Approve & Pay Deposit" (requires the "Require client signatures" setting). On the web staff use "Collect Deposit" (or "Record Deposit" without Jobber Payments). The app accepts saved cards, new cards, Tap to Pay, card reader and offline methods. Clients cannot part-pay a quote deposit. — [Deposits on quotes](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/deposits-on-quotes)
- Deposits tied to a converted quote apply automatically when the invoice is generated; other deposits sit on the client's account balance and can be applied manually to any outstanding invoice for that client. Offline deposits can be edited/deleted; Jobber Payments deposits cannot. — [Deposits on quotes](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/deposits-on-quotes); [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- Deposits move only between invoices of the same client (remove from one, add to the other); they cannot move between quotes. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)
- The billing screen cannot charge a card for a free-standing deposit; card deposits must be collected on a quote. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- With automatic payments on a recurring job, quote deposits carry forward and are applied to the first automatic invoice. — [Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments)

**Payment schedules / progress invoicing**
- Available on all plans. A schedule splits one job into several progress invoices, defined on the quote or on a one-off job (not recurring jobs), by percentage or fixed amounts with a description per instalment; instalments must total 100% / the full amount before saving. The first instalment can be flagged "Required quote deposit". — [Payment schedules and progress invoicing](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/progress-invoicing)
- The job's Billing section shows a progress bar (green paid, yellow awaiting payment, dark grey draft, light grey remaining) and a per-instalment list with due date, status, percentage, description, total, balance and a Create button. — [Payment schedules and progress invoicing](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/progress-invoicing)
- Each progress invoice takes its share of any job discount; tax is computed on the discounted amount at the job's rate; line-item amounts on progress invoices cannot be edited (change the job, then re-balance remaining instalments); issued invoices are locked. The progress number is appended to the subject. — [Payment schedules and progress invoicing](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/progress-invoicing)
- Edge case: on a job-defined schedule instalments start at 0%, so creating an invoice before entering amounts yields a $0 invoice that must be deleted and recreated. — [Payment schedules and progress invoicing](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/progress-invoicing)

**Credits, account balance, initial balance, statements**
- Client profile Billing section lists invoices, payments and deposits with record type/number, "Applied to", created date and amount, and a Current balance row. Positive balance means the client owes; negative means credit/overpayment (for example a deposit before any invoice). Tips show in history but are excluded from the balance. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- The + menu on Billing offers: Collect Payment, Record Deposit, Invoice, Statement, Set Initial Balance (a pre-Jobber opening balance with amount and details, paid down by later payments/deposits). — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- A payment collected against an invoice stays linked to it and cannot be moved; if that invoice is deleted the payment returns to the account balance. Jobber Payments transactions cannot be edited (support must move them). Editable fields on offline records: transaction date, method, details, "Applied to". Deleted payments cannot be recovered. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- Statements: three ranges (Client Lifetime, Since Last Zero Balance, Pick a Start Time); Download/Preview or Email to Client (template editable); columns Date, Description, Amount, Payment, Tip, running Balance; for reference only, as clients pay individual invoices not the statement; outstanding balance includes all issued invoices whether or not past due; transaction order is set in Work Settings. — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- Receipts: Send Receipt from the payment, or Download PDF; Jobber Payments card receipts can auto-send (toggle in Jobber Payments settings). — [Client billing history](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-billing-history)
- Referral credits (Marketing Suite / Plus) are a second credit mechanism: one credit per invoice, oldest unused credit auto-applied to most invoice types. See section 5. — [Referrals | Marketing Tools](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/referrals-marketing-tools)

**Refunds**
- Jobber Payments refunds are issued from the payment details page (More > Issue refund), web only: full, or "Other amount" for partial, with an optional "Refund the tip". Bulk payments covering several invoices are refunded one invoice portion at a time. — [Jobber Payments refunds](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-refunds)
- Refunds take 5-10 business days to reach the client, cannot be cancelled, and the original processing fees are returned to the business. Refunds draw on the Jobber Payments balance; below -$1,000 refunds are disabled until the balance recovers. — [Jobber Payments refunds](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-refunds)
- After a refund, the invoice is not automatically reopened: choose Re-open Invoice (balance returns to the account), Collect Payment for a different amount, or close as Bad Debt. Refund receipts must be sent manually. Billing history shows "Refunded" / "Partially Refunded". — [Jobber Payments refunds](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-refunds)
- Offline payments cannot be refunded in Jobber: refund outside, then edit the original payment to $0.00 with a note. — [Jobber Payments refunds](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-refunds)

**Taxes**
- Tax rates are created in Company Settings (name + rate); one default; new properties inherit the default, existing ones do not change. A Tax ID name (the article's example is GST) and number print at the bottom-left of all invoices. — [Tax settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tax-settings)
- Tax groups combine existing rates; the group rate is the sum and each component shows separately on quotes and invoices; a group can be the default. — [Tax settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tax-settings)
- Tax is per property: each property either follows the global default (and tracks future default changes) or has a custom rate. Resolution: quote uses the property's rate; job from a quote uses the quote's rate; standalone job uses the property's rate; invoice from a job uses the job's rate (none on job means none on invoice); invoice without a job uses the property's rate. Once a job has a rate, later quote/property/default changes do not affect it, and changing the job's rate only affects invoices created afterwards. Rate can be overridden or removed on any single invoice. — [Tax settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tax-settings)
- Viewing or changing a job's tax or discount requires the "Show pricing" permission. — [Tax settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tax-settings); [Discounts](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/discounts)
- Taxation report offers Accrual (invoices marked as sent, with Bad Debt in a separate section) and Cash (payments collected in the period, with an "Unallocated Revenue" section for payments not applied to an invoice and for overpayments); multiple tax rates are listed on both. — [Taxation report](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/taxation-report)

**Discounts**
- Global discount: flat $ or % off the whole quote, job or invoice, deducted from the subtotal. Alternatively, discount line items with a negative unit price (several allowed, each named). — [Discounts](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/discounts)
- Calculation order: each line total rounded to 2 decimals; taxable and non-taxable lines subtotalled separately; discounts come off the non-taxable subtotal first and any remainder off the taxable subtotal; tax computed on the remaining taxable amount; final total rounded. Article example: $300 subtotal, 15% discount, 5% tax gives $267.75. — [Discounts](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/discounts)
- Carry-over: quote global discount carries to the job, and to every invoice created from the job (including each invoice of a recurring job). Changing the job's discount affects only later invoices. Changing discount or tax on an invoice does not change the job. — [Discounts](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/discounts); [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)

**Bad debt**
- More > Close Invoice > As Bad Debt. For a partial write-off, record the partial payment first; only the remaining balance is written off. The written-off amount is removed from the client's account balance; date and amount show under the invoice total. "Unmark as bad debt" reopens it. — [Bad debt](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bad-debt)
- Clients are not notified; bad-debt invoices disappear from client hub; email/text are disabled for them; the PDF shows a $0.00 balance without indicating bad debt. A Bad Debt report (a filtered view of the Invoices report) lists them. — [Bad debt](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bad-debt)

**Templates, branding, PDF**
- Client document settings (Business Profile) control all PDFs. Style: header layout (Basic, Compact, #10 Envelope single window, #10 Envelope dual window), header style (Modern or Clean), logo size (S/M/L), header colour, footer font size, toggles for company phone/email/website and for client phone and client-level custom fields. — [Client document settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-document-settings)
- Invoice tab: show quantity / unit price / line total; return payment stub on unpaid invoices (sized for #8 envelopes); late stamp on overdue invoices; account balance; paid date; which custom fields are client-facing; contract/disclaimer. Quote tab adds: relabel "Quote" as "Estimate" everywhere, footer totals/tax, deposit language, client signature line. Job tab: signature line, custom fields, disclaimer. — [Client document settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-document-settings)
- Changes affect only PDFs created afterwards. PDFs are not attached to emails by default; clients download from client hub, or staff tick the attachment per email; total email attachments under 10 MB. — [Client document settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-document-settings)
- Invoice emails include a client hub link and a pay button when Jobber Payments is active; the payment-options block is auto-added and not editable. Invoice texts go only to numbers in the account's country; the client hub link in a text expires after 24 hours, after which the client signs in with the last four digits of their phone number. — [Invoice basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/invoice-basics)

### Inferences
- The core mechanism a basic CRM is most likely missing is the **invoice-reminder -> Requires Invoicing -> batch create (drafts) -> batch deliver** loop, together with the per-job billing choice (per visit vs fixed price) and invoicing schedule (after each visit / on close / month-end / custom / none). For a snow-removal crew billing monthly or per push, this loop is the main workflow.
- Tax is modelled on the **property**, snapshotted onto quote/job/invoice at creation. For Calgary a single 5% GST rate suffices, and tax groups exist for GST+PST provinces (the help article does not give that example explicitly).
- Jobber appears to have no computed late fee or interest; parity only requires a late stamp, a disclaimer, two past-due follow-ups and manual fee line items. A real late-fee engine would exceed Jobber here.
- "Close without payment" and "payment applied to account balance" show Jobber tolerates invoice status and ledger balance diverging; the client-balance-summary troubleshooting note confirms this causes mismatches. A parity build may prefer to forbid the divergence.

### Gaps
- Exactly how each term's due date is computed (from issued date vs sent date) and the exact moment an invoice flips to Past Due are not stated in the articles read.
- Whether automatic late fees were added in 2026: I found no Help Center article describing one; the only direct statement is an undated community thread.
- Tax-inclusive pricing, tax-exempt clients, and per-line tax overrides beyond the taxable flag are not covered in the tax article.
- Batch invoice count limits and whether batch delivery by text exists were not stated.
- I did not read the "Snow removal workflow" article body or the "Import invoices" article.

## 2. Payments: Jobber Payments features, Canada availability, card on file, automatic payments, ACH, Tap to Pay, tips, surcharges, instant payouts, financing, disputes, reports

### Takeaway
Jobber Payments (Stripe-backed, included on every plan) is available in the US, Canada and the UK and covers online card payments via client hub, Apple Pay/Google Pay, cards on file, automatic charging on recurring jobs (Connect and up), Tap to Pay, a card reader, tips, in-app refunds, a disputes dashboard and instant payouts. For a Calgary business the important exclusions are: **no ACH/bank payments, no Wisetack consumer financing, no cheque scan, no Jobber Money, no Interac on Tap to Pay**, and a roughly $250 contactless ceiling on Tap to Pay. I found no surcharge/convenience-fee feature documented.

### Cited Findings

**Basics and availability**
- Jobber Payments is included with the subscription and available in the UK, US and Canada; accepts cards with Visa, Mastercard, Amex or Discover logos; Apple Pay and Google Pay in client hub; minimum payment $0.50. — [Jobber Payments basics](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-basics)
- **[US-only]** Bank payments (ACH) are only available to US accounts. — [Bank payments (ACH)](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bank-payments-ach)

- A default method (star + "Default") is suggested at charge time. - UK only: staff cannot key in cards (SCA); clients must save their own. — [Saving and charging payment methods](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/saving-and-charging-payment-methods-with-jobber-payments)

**Automatic payments on recurring jobs** **[CA ok]**
- Plans: Connect, Grow, Plus. Regions: US and Canada (plus UK). Off by default; enabled per recurring job with "Get paid automatically" in the Invoicing & automatic payments section; needs a saved method or sends a request, and starts once the client adds one. — [Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments)
- Billing styles: per visit (invoice lists each visit's line items) or fixed price (same amount each time). "As needed" is incompatible. — [Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments)
- Failure handling: one attempt, no retries; admins get an email and activity-feed entry; the invoice shows a banner and stays unpaid; a new method supplied via Collect Payment is used for this and future charges. On success the client is emailed a receipt with a view-invoice button. — [Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments)
- Side effects: no invoice reminders are generated; manual invoice creation is removed for those jobs; referral credits are not applied to automatic invoices; the recurring jobs report gains an "Automatic invoicing" column; users who can merely complete visits can thereby trigger auto-invoicing on per-visit jobs. — [Automatic payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automatic-payments)

**ACH** **[US-only]**
- 1% fee netted from the payout; 4-business-day settlement on top of the payout window; Plaid bank login (12,000+ institutions) or manual entry; $0.01 penny test; payments can show as successful then fail in settlement; refunds only after payout, 9-15 calendar days, not after 90 days; ACH disputes cannot be contested. — [Bank payments (ACH)](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bank-payments-ach)

**Tap to Pay** **[CA differs]**

- **Canada:** Interac is not supported, and transactions over $250 may exceed tap limits and then need a physical reader (offline PIN) or manual entry. — [Tap to Pay](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tap-to-pay)
- Flow: Collect Deposit / Collect Invoice > Tap to Pay; client reviews, may add a tip, taps; on failure staff can retry, switch method, or "Request Online Payment" (sends a client hub link). iPhone requires an owner/admin to accept Apple's terms once. Saving a card on file is not possible from a tap. Needs internet. Required permissions: edit own schedule, full client/property, jobs, invoices, show pricing. — [Tap to Pay](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tap-to-pay)

Tips can be refunded alone or with the payment. — [Tip collection with Jobber Payments](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/tip-collection-with-jobber-payments)

**Instant payouts** **[CA differs]**
- US and Canada; all plans but eligibility is decided by Jobber from processing history; owner-only. Extra 1% deducted from the payout; up to $9,999 per withdrawal and $10,000 per 24 hours; usually within 30 minutes, any day. **Canada requires a Visa or Mastercard debit card, not Interac.** Adding/changing the card has a 48-hour verification period. - Only for clients and businesses in the US; additionally, snow removal (with pool & spa, residential/commercial cleaning, window washing) is an excluded industry. Quotes $500-$65,000; residential clients only; one-off work completed within 90 days; merchant fee 3.9%; terms 3-120 months at 0%-35.9% APR. — [Jobber and Wisetack consumer financing integration](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-wisetack-consumer-financing-integration)

**Disputes**

### Inferences
- For a Calgary crew, parity on payments means: online card pay page with Apple/Google Pay, card on file, auto-charge on recurring jobs with month-end 9 PM batch behaviour, tips, tap to pay, refunds, and a payout ledger. ## 3. Client Hub: everything a client can do

### Takeaway
Client hub is a passwordless, auto-provisioned portal on all plans where a client can submit requests/bookings, approve quotes (optionally with signature) or request changes, pay deposits and invoices (with tip, partial amount if enabled), see and confirm appointments, manage saved cards in a Wallet, download PDFs/receipts, see contact info, and refer a friend. Links cannot be revoked, and anyone holding a link sees what the client sees.

### Cited Findings
- Access: passwordless link sent by email or text; anyone with the link (including CC'd recipients) sees the same information; links cannot be deactivated or reset, even by Jobber Support. If more than 14 days have passed since the last message containing a link, the client may be asked for their email address or the last four digits of their phone number. Staff can "Send Login Email" (not customisable, no text version) and can embed a client hub login link/button on their website. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- Links in text messages expire after 24 hours; the client can still sign in with the last 4 digits of their phone number. — [Quote approvals](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quote-approvals)
- **Requests:** client submits the default request form; submissions remain visible in the hub; requests created internally do not appear; a payment-method field on the form saves a card to Wallet. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- **Quotes:** shown as Awaiting Response or Approved (converted quotes count as Approved); drafts and archived hidden; quotes do not expire unless archived; client can approve, request changes (if enabled), pay a required deposit, download PDF. Optional line items and line-item images need Grow. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- Approval: drawn or typed signature when "Require client signatures" is on; button becomes "Approve and Pay Deposit" when a deposit is required; signature saved as an internal note. Request Changes moves the quote to "Changes requested" and hides it from the hub until staff amend and resend or set it back to Awaiting Response. Client gets an automatic approval email; admins get an activity-feed alert and push notification. Editing an approved quote's total, deposit, line items, message, contract or number removes the signature (prior signed version kept as a PDF note). — [Quote approvals](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quote-approvals)
- **Appointments:** all past appointments (five at a time, Show More) and the next five upcoming within five years; shows date, time or arrival window, address, assigned team members' first names and photos/initials; "Confirm Appointment" marks the visit confirmed (thumbs-up icon in Jobber). A setting can hide times and show date only. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub); [Booking confirmation](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/booking-confirmation)
- **Invoices:** Awaiting Payment, Paid and Past Due visible; Draft and Bad Debt hidden; pay online, add a tip, pay a partial amount where enabled, download PDF; on mobile the payment form sits above the invoice. Receipts are available in the hub as downloadable PDFs. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub); [Bank payments (ACH)](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bank-payments-ach)
- **Wallet:** add cards, set default, see methods added by staff, remove (except the only card on an automatic-payment job). — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- **Contact Us:** company name, phone, website, email, address, business hours, social links from Company Settings / Business Profile. — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- **Refer a Friend:** all plans; default ON; adds an invoice banner and a nav tab; opens a share pop-up with a link to the default request form and an editable default message; resulting requests carry a Referral field linking to the referrer. No reward logic in this basic version. — [Client hub settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-hub-settings); [Referrals in client hub](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/referrals-in-client-hub)
- Settings (all plans): Quotes and Invoices menu visibility toggle (off means documents open only from their own email/text link); Require client signatures; Clients can request changes; Show scheduled time; "Include Client Hub links in appointment messages" (in Emails & Text settings). — [Client hub settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-hub-settings)
- Staff tools: "Log in as Client" delegate view (cannot pay or submit requests) and per-document "Preview as client". — [What do your clients see in client hub?](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub)
- Client hub reflects document settings (estimate label, shown/hidden quantities and prices, custom fields, contract text) and the logo; "Keep address private" removes the company address. — [Client document settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/client-document-settings)

### Inferences
- The client cannot reschedule or cancel appointments themselves in client hub per these articles (rescheduling/cancelling is only described through the AI Receptionist). A self-serve reschedule would exceed Jobber.
- The irrevocable, shareable-link security model is a deliberate convenience trade-off; a parity build can copy the convenience but add link expiry or revocation cheaply.

### Gaps
- No article described clients viewing job photos, checklists/reports, or messaging the business inside client hub; I cannot confirm absence, only that it is undocumented in the pages read.
- Custom domain or deeper branding for client hub is not covered.
- I did not read "A guide for your clients: How to navigate client hub".

## 4. Communications: templates, two-way texting, dedicated number, message centre, call tracking, automated notifications, variables

### Takeaway
Every plan can send templated emails and texts for requests, quotes, jobs and invoices with `{{VARIABLE}}` merge fields; Connect and up add scheduled visit/assessment reminders (max two per type), job-closure follow-ups with a 1-10 survey, and quote/invoice follow-ups. Two-way SMS, a dedicated number and the reply-capable Message Center are Grow/Plus; lower tiers get a read-only "Comms" log. Text messaging works in Canada; the A2P 10DLC registration burden is US-only. Call tracking is not native; it comes through the CallRail integration or the AI Receptionist.

### Cited Findings

**Templates and channels**
- Settings > Emails & Text Messages is admin-only and exists on all plans; text messaging is available in the US, Canada and UK, only to numbers in the account's country. — [Emails and text messages settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/emails-and-text-messages-settings)
- Template inventory: Requests (submitted-request auto-email; booking confirmation email/text; assessment reminders email/text). Quotes (new quote email/text; quote approval auto-email). Jobs (booking confirmation email/text; visit reminder email/text; visit rescheduling notification; checklist email; chemical treatment email; job follow-up email). Invoices (new invoice email/text; payment and deposit receipt email). General (statement email; request a payment method on file email/text; signed documents email). — [Emails and text messages settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/emails-and-text-messages-settings)
- Emails can be edited per send without changing the template; text previews cannot be edited per message (change the template). Text limit 160 characters per segment; longer texts split. — [Emails and text messages settings](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/emails-and-text-messages-settings)
- Sender addresses: uncustomised client templates come from notification@msg.getjobber.com, customised ones from notification@txn.getjobber.com. ### Inferences
- A basic CRM likely has one reminder; Jobber parity needs: two reminders per type with relative/fixed timing, reply-to-confirm ("Yes"/"C"), a reschedule "Notify client?" prompt, per-contact opt-out flags per notification class, and a communications log with open/bounce status.

## 6. Automations: builder, follow-ups, tasks, limits

### Takeaway
Jobber has two layers: fixed, pre-built automations on Connect and up (quote follow-ups, invoice follow-ups, auto-archive, AI auto-drafted quotes, high-value quote alerts, visit reminders, job follow-ups) and a narrow custom automation builder on Grow/Plus that only works on **quotes and invoices**, with date-based triggers, up to six conditions, and three actions (notify client, add client tag, archive quote).

### Cited Findings
- Automations page (Settings > Automations, admin-only). Pre-built set: Auto-archive requests (after N days unconverted); Quote follow-up (two); Auto-archive quotes (after N days still awaiting response); Auto-draft quotes from new requests (Jobber AI using templates, request details and past quotes; suggested after three or more quotes sent via "Draft for me"); High-value quote alerts (push notification when an auto-drafted quote exceeds a threshold, default the average quote value; requires auto-draft); Invoice follow-up (two). Plans: Connect, Grow, Plus. — [Automations](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automations)
- Custom automation builder: Grow and Plus. Structure: title, one trigger, optional conditions, one action; saving makes it live immediately. — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Triggers: "Date passed" (X days after quotes are sent or approved; X days after invoices are paid or due) and "Date arrives" (morning or evening when quotes are sent/approved or invoices paid/due). Morning is 8 AM and evening 7 PM local. Day counts up to 90. — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Conditions (max six): Status is / is not (quote: Awaiting response, Changes requested, Approved, Converted, Archived; invoice: Due, Paid); Client tag includes / does not include; Line items include / do not include any item from Products & Services. — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Actions: Notify (client email or text, same method as the original quote/invoice); Add tag (to the client); Update status (quote to Archived only). — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Not retroactive: a rule fires only when an item is exactly N days into the state, so pre-existing older items are skipped. — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Suggested recipes: archive stale quotes (30 days); tag delinquent clients "non-payment"; extra follow-ups; VIP tagging by line item; VIP/new-client tailored follow-ups; post-service maintenance reminder (X days after a paid invoice containing a given service). — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder)
- Plan conflict: the Help Center says the builder is Grow and Plus, and the pricing page lists "Build custom workflow automations" under Grow; an older product-update post (via search snippet) says Plus. — [Custom automation builder](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/custom-automation-builder); [Jobber pricing](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/); contradicted by [Product update: custom automation builder](https://productupdates.getjobber.com/76491-save-time-and-tailor-jobber-to-your-needs-with-the-custom-automation-builder)
- Other built-in event automation: the AI Receptionist creates follow-up tasks when it takes a message (assigned to admins, dated same day or next day after hours). — [Receptionist, powered by Jobber AI](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai)
- External automation: Zapier (Connect and up) exposes 11 triggers and 6 actions; see section 9. — [Jobber and Zapier integration](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-zapier-integration)

### Inferences
- The builder cannot trigger on jobs, visits, requests, payments or weather, and cannot create tasks, jobs or invoices. For snow removal (event-driven by snowfall), Jobber's native automation offers nothing; a parity build already matches it with date-offset rules on quotes/invoices, and anything visit- or weather-triggered would exceed it.
- The "post-service maintenance reminder" recipe (X days after paid invoice containing service Y, max 90 days) is Jobber's only native seasonal re-engagement automation outside Campaigns; the 90-day ceiling makes it unsuitable for annual renewals, which Campaigns' "time since last job" trigger covers instead.

### Gaps
- Maximum number of custom automations per account is not stated.
- Whether tasks can be created by automations (other than by Receptionist) is not documented; no "create task" action exists in the builder article.
